How pricing actually works

Revisions

Two rounds are included in every package. Anything past that is billed per revision — agreed before work starts, not after.

Advance

New clients pay 50% upfront. It protects both sides and it's non-negotiable for first-time projects.

Retainers

Need this every week? Monthly retainers get priority turnaround and a lower effective rate than one-off orders.

Custom scope

Longer videos, bigger batches, or a mix of services — send the details and get a fixed quote before anything starts.
